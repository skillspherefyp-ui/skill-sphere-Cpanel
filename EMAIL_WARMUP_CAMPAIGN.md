- Send at the same time each day (9am or 6pm works best)
- After each send, message your users on WhatsApp or in-app to check their email
- Ask them to REPLY to the email — a reply is the strongest trust signal for Gmail
- Day 5, 14, 17, and 20 are your most important days — they invite replies which boost reputation fastest
- Do not skip days — consistency matters more than content
- Week 3 should show clear improvement in inbox delivery for new accounts
