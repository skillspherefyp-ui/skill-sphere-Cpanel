Abstract

SkillSphere is a full-stack, AI-powered Learning Management System built with React Native Web and Node.js, supporting four roles: Student, Expert, Instructor, and Admin. The platform integrates OpenAI GPT-4o to deliver intelligent lecture generation, real-time AI tutoring, text-to-speech, voice interaction, AI-generated flashcards & quizzes, and automated PDF certificates upon completion. Students benefit from progress tracking, learning streaks, and discussion forums — creating a complete, gamified, and personalized learning experience accessible on web and mobile.
